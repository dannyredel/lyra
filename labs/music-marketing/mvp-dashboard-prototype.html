Metrics

Your bank of unified metrics. Every metric shows which source each term comes from — so "ROAS" is never ambiguous. Two kinds: Observed (arithmetic over connected sources) and Causal (produced by a model run).

Composition is explicit by design: the same transparency promise the model makes, the metric layer makes too. Field harmonization (mapping each platform's raw "cost / spend / value" naming into one unified field) happens earlier, at the connector — so here you only ever compose already-clean fields.
