The workhorse · causal inference

Prediction guesses what happens next. Causal inference proves what you caused.

We build the counterfactual — what would have happened without the campaign — and measure the gap. That gap is the effect: the average treatment effect, or ATT.

Prediction"Sales will rise." Correlation, forecasting — useful, but blind to cause.
Causal"Your spend is why they rose — by this much." The number you can act on.
— treated (actual) · - - synthetic control · the gap after the line = the effect
